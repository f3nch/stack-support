Stack: Encrypted Notes — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Contact: f3nch@proton.me

The short version

Stack doesn't collect, transmit or sell any of your data. Your notes stay on your iPhone, encrypted. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

What Stack stores, and where

  • Your notes, sets and dividers. Note titles, note text, set names and divider names are encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before they're saved. Set colours, the order of sets, dividers and notes, which divider a note is filed behind, and dates are stored alongside them, unencrypted, so the app can sort and display your notes. Everything is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection.
  • Your encryption key. A random key is created on your iPhone the first time you use Stack. It's kept in the iOS Keychain, can only be used after Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, and is never backed up to another device or synced.
  • One setting that contains no note content: whether you've seen the welcome screen.

Stack has no server. We (the developer) never receive your notes, your key or information about how you use the app.

Face ID and Touch ID

Stack asks iOS to confirm it's you before it unlocks your notes. iOS handles the check; Stack never sees or stores your face or fingerprint data.

Sharing

When you share a note or set, Stack creates an encrypted file protected by a passphrase you can see and change. You choose where the file goes (AirDrop, Messages, Mail, Files and so on), and that app's own privacy policy applies to it. Anyone who has both the file and the passphrase can read it, so send the passphrase separately.

If you choose Share as Plain Text, the note leaves Stack unencrypted, and you're warned before that happens.

Backups and recovery

Because the key never leaves your iPhone, notes can't be recovered if you lose or erase the device or remove its passcode. Stack doesn't have a backup feature yet.

Children

Stack isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
