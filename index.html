Stack: Encrypted Notes — Support

Stack keeps your notes as a stack of cards, encrypted on your iPhone. Questions, bug reports and feature ideas are welcome: email f3nch@proton.me and include your iOS version and what you were doing.

Common questions

  • Can I get my notes back on a new phone? Not yet. The encryption key never leaves your iPhone and isn't backed up, so notes can't be recovered if the phone is lost, erased or its passcode is removed. To keep a copy, share notes or sets as encrypted files and store the file and passphrase somewhere safe.
  • Why does Stack ask for Face ID? To unlock the key that decrypts your notes. Stack relocks after you've been away for a minute. Your passcode works too.
  • How do I share a note? Tap the share button in a note (or a card's ⋯ menu). Stack makes an encrypted file and shows a passphrase. Send the passphrase a different way from the file.
  • How do dividers work? Use ⋯ → New Divider, then long-press a note and drag it onto the brown divider tab. Tap a divider's tab to rename or delete it; deleting a divider never deletes its notes.
  • Does Stack collect any data? No. There are no accounts, analytics or ads. See the privacy policy.
